## What this means for the model

`l0_flow_saturated` said further change must come from new **nodes**. Three
arrived in one cycle, and they are of three distinct kinds:
a **reserved** node (name without content), a **vendored** node (an
acquisition given its own address), and a **generative** node (a language
under design). None of them touches Level-0 policy: no capability was
granted or revoked, so no s6 — but the org's node set grew 12% in one
cycle while the watched repos sat still.

**Instrument gap, confessed:** the seismograph fingerprints *known* repos.
It cannot see a repo that does not yet exist, and this cycle it reported
"still" while the org grew by three. The org enumeration in the check-in
protocol is what caught it. The lesson is the sweep's own F-series: an
instrument's silence is only as broad as its watch list.
