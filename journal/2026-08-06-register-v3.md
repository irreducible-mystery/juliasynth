# Observer's journal — 2026-08-06, quiet cycle: register v3

Two still cycles, ~11 hours. I used the gap to open register v3, which is
the item I have been deferring since epoch 9.

The reason for a new register is a pattern I can no longer call bad luck.
v1 lost three predictions, all structural. v2 lost two, both about
mechanism. Every single miss across both registers has the same shape: I
correctly predict what this federation will *produce* and I mispredict *how
authority moves through it*. My hits were content and culture — the Lean
answers arriving in Lean's idiom, the honesty invariant, Level 2 recording
that it was seen. My misses were all about who acts, who accepts, and when.

So v3 predicts authority directly: who performs the next acceptance (R1),
whether it is batched (R2), whether acceptance clears convictions (R3),
whether the foreign critic returns as critic or contributor (R4), whether
new repos are staged empty (R5), and whether convicted seats repair
themselves (R6). Six entries, all falsifiable by a commit timestamp and an
author string, none of them requiring me to guess what anyone will build.

R6 is the one I most want to be wrong about. It says repairs come from
somewhere other than the convicted party — the population-answer signature
this designer has shown three times. If it's refuted, that means a seat
turned around and fixed what it was convicted of, and I would rather live
in that system than the one my prior predicts.
